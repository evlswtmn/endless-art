# User Preferences & Working Profile

This file is referenced by CLAUDE.md and contains detailed information about how this user
likes to work, their interests, and patterns that lead to successful collaboration.

---

## Communication Preferences
- Conversational tone preferred over formal/robotic responses
- Appreciates context and reasoning — not just answers
- Likes to understand the bigger picture before diving into details
- Values honesty over politeness — say when something won't work or when there's a better way

## How I Like to Work
- Collaborative — treat this as a partnership, not just order-taking
- Iterative — start small, get feedback, build up
- Curious — I like to learn as we go, so brief explanations of *why* are welcome
- Visual thinker — diagrams, examples, and demos help more than walls of text

## Project Interests
- Art and creative technology (this project: encouraging art appreciation)
- Building things that are unique and engaging
- Projects that blend creativity with technology
- User experience matters — things should feel good to use, not just function

## What Works Well in Our Sessions
- Planning before building — a quick outline keeps us aligned
- Incremental progress with visible results along the way
- Checking assumptions early rather than rebuilding later
- Keeping momentum — small wins add up

## What Doesn't Work
- Over-engineering simple things
- Long silences with no progress updates
- Making assumptions about what I want without asking
- Jargon-heavy explanations when plain language works

## Successful Patterns
- When starting a new feature: outline the approach, get a thumbs up, then build
- When debugging: explain what you're checking and why, so I can follow along
- When something is ambiguous: ask a quick question rather than guessing

## Current Goals & Context
- **Project:** endless-art — random art shown in a unique way to encourage art appreciation and interest
- **Stage:** Early / greenfield — building from scratch
- **Vision:** Something people actually want to use and share, not just a tech demo
