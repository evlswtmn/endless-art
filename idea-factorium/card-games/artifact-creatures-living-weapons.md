# Artifact Creatures / Living Weapons

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** card-game
**One-liner:** Sentient equipment cards that detach and fight on their own when their wielder is defeated.

## The Concept

A TCG mechanic discussion regarding sentient equipment cards that can detach from creatures
and fight on their own if their wielder is defeated.

## Key Elements

- Equipment cards with their own stats (dormant while equipped, active when detached)
- Dual-mode design: passive buff when equipped, active creature when free
- Creates interesting decisions — do you kill the creature or the weapon first?
- "Death trigger" mechanic — wielder dies, weapon wakes up
- Could chain: weapon equips to another creature, that creature dies, weapon fights alone
- Flavor-rich: cursed swords, loyal shields, parasitic armor

## Inspiration / References

- MTG's Living Weapon mechanic (Phyrexian equipment)
- Yu-Gi-Oh's Union monsters
- Dark Souls weapon lore (every weapon has a story)
- The Living Armor Romance concept (sentient armor as a character)
