# The "Burnout" Mechanic

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** card-game
**One-liner:** Creatures get a massive stat boost now but are automatically destroyed on your opponent's next turn.

## The Concept

A risk/reward TCG mechanic. Creatures with Burnout get a massive stat boost immediately,
but are automatically destroyed (or transformed into a weaker state) during your opponent's
next turn. High risk, high reward aggro strategy.

## Key Elements

- Risk/reward tempo mechanic
- Massive immediate power at the cost of guaranteed loss next turn
- Enables explosive aggro strategies
- Creates tension: do you Burnout for the kill or play it safe?
- "Transformed into a weaker state" variant adds nuance (not just dead, but diminished)
- Could have cards that interact with Burnout (prevent it, trigger it on enemies, benefit from ally deaths)

## Inspiration / References

- MTG's "Ball Lightning" style creatures (enters, hits hard, dies)
- Hearthstone's charge/rush mechanics (immediate impact)
- Berserker tropes in fantasy (overexertion for power)
- Real-world "burnout" as a metaphor (going too hard, crashing)

## Open Questions

- Destroyed or transformed? (Destroyed is cleaner; transformed adds depth)
- What's the stat boost multiplier that makes it worth the risk?
- Does Burnout trigger at start of opponent's turn or end?
- Can Burnout be "healed" or prevented by support cards?
- How does this interact with the Living Weapons concept?
- Is this for a custom TCG or a mechanic proposal for an existing game?
