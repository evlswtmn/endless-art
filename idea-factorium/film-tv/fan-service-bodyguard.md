# The "Fan Service" Bodyguard

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** film
**One-liner:** A washed-up actor hired as a pop star's body double accidentally becomes the target of real assassins.

## The Concept

A movie pitch about a washed-up actor who is hired to be a "Body Actor" (stunt double)
for a massive pop star. The plot is a thriller where assassins are after the star, but the
actor keeps getting mistaken for the star due to the body double work.

## Key Elements

- Mistaken identity thriller with a Hollywood/celebrity backdrop
- Washed-up actor character — desperate enough to take the job, skilled enough to survive
- The body double role becomes literally life-threatening
- Commentary on celebrity culture, identity, and who the "real" person is
- Action/thriller with dark comedy potential
- The actor's acting skills become survival skills

## Inspiration / References

- The Bodyguard (1992) — but from the double's perspective
- Dave (1993) — ordinary person standing in for a powerful figure
- John Wick (reluctant action hero)
- Tropic Thunder (actor in a real dangerous situation)

## Open Questions

- Comedy-thriller or straight thriller?
- Does the actor develop a relationship with the pop star?
- Is the pop star aware of the danger or complicit somehow?
- What genre of pop star — K-pop, Western pop, regional?
- Does the actor's career get revived or destroyed by the experience?
