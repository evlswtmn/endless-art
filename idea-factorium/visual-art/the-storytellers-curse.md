# The Storyteller's Curse

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** visual-art
**One-liner:** An author can write things into existence but can never write himself a happy ending.

## The Concept

A short story/film concept. An author has the power to write things into existence, but he
cannot write himself a happy ending. Every time he tries to insert himself into the
narrative, the universe course-corrects to maintain his suffering.

## Key Elements

- Meta-narrative — a writer whose power is writing, but limited for himself
- The universe as an editor that rejects his self-inserts
- Tragic premise with potential for dark comedy
- Could visualize the "course corrections" as literal narrative interventions
- Explores the idea that creators pour themselves into work but can't fix their own lives
- The curse could be metaphorical (depression, imposter syndrome) or literal

## Inspiration / References

- Stranger Than Fiction (character aware of being written)
- The Neverending Story (writer affecting/entering the narrative)
- Adaptation (Charlie Kaufman writing himself into failure)
- Alan Wake (writer whose fiction becomes real)
