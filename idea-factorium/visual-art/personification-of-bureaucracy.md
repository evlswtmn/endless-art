# The Anthropomorphic Personification of Bureaucracy

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** visual-art
**One-liner:** Not the Grim Reaper — the entity that files you in the wrong department for eternity.

## The Concept

A visual character design concept. Not the grim reaper, but the personification of
paperwork, lost forms, and red tape. An entity that doesn't kill you — it just files you
in the wrong department for eternity.

## Key Elements

- Character design exercise / potential recurring character
- The horror of bureaucracy made literal
- Visual language: suits, stamps, filing cabinets, infinite hallways
- Not malicious — just doing its job, which happens to be eternal
- Could be terrifying, funny, or both depending on execution
- "Death takes you once. Bureaucracy takes you forever."

## Inspiration / References

- Terry Pratchett's Death (Discworld) — personification with personality
- Brazil (1985 film) — bureaucratic dystopia
- The Adjustment Bureau — cosmic administrative forces
- Spirited Away (Yubaba's bathhouse bureaucracy)
- Kafka (The Trial, The Castle)
