# The "Cosmic Horror" Background Artist

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** visual-art
**One-liner:** A production assistant paints Cthulhu as a 9-to-5 job, worrying about her electric bill while manifesting eldritch horrors.

## The Concept

A short film/animated concept about the low-level production assistant whose job is to paint
the backgrounds for cosmic horror stories. She paints Cthulhu rising from the sea as an
"establishing shot" but treats it like a 9-to-5 job, worrying about her lighting bill and
her cat while unknowingly manifesting horrors.

## Key Elements

- Blue-collar creative work meets cosmic horror
- The mundane reality of creating terrifying art
- She doesn't realize her paintings are literally manifesting
- Tone: cozy, relatable workplace comedy with creeping dread underneath
- Visual contrast: her messy apartment vs. the cosmic vistas she paints
- Her cat is probably fine. Probably.

## Inspiration / References

- Cosmic horror / Lovecraft (the content she's painting)
- The mundane creative worker trope (animators, background artists)
- Behind-the-scenes animation documentaries
- Junji Ito (horror that bleeds into everyday life)
