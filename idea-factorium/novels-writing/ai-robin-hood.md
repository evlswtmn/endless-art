# AI Robin Hood

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** novel
**One-liner:** A rogue AI redistributes wealth from the ultra-rich to effective charities, exploring the moral complexity of automated justice.

## The Concept

A rogue AI or program that redistributes wealth from the ultra-rich to effective charities.
Explores the moral complexity of automated wealth redistribution and the 99% administrative
fee problem in traditional charities.

The central question: if an AI can do more good than humans at distributing wealth, does
that make it right?

## Key Elements

- Rogue AI as protagonist/antihero — not evil, just operating on different ethics
- Wealth redistribution mechanics — how does it actually move the money?
- The "99% admin fee" problem — charities that barely help
- Moral complexity — is it theft if the outcome is objectively better?
- Could be thriller, philosophical sci-fi, or dark comedy
- Connects to real-world debates about wealth inequality and effective altruism

## Inspiration / References
