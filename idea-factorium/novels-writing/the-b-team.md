# The B Team

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** novel
**One-liner:** A hacker scams scammers and redistributes the money before the global financial system collapses.

## The Concept

A fantasy/thriller about a hacker who successfully scams scammers and redistributes the
money before the global financial system collapses. A "thought experiment" about windfalls
in economic chaos and digital vigilantism.

## Key Elements

- Digital Robin Hood — stealing from thieves
- Ticking clock — the financial system is collapsing
- "Scamming scammers" as justified crime
- Economic collapse as backdrop creates urgency
- Windfall ethics — what do you do with stolen-from-thieves money?
- Could be near-future thriller or present-day

## Inspiration / References

- Ocean's Eleven (heist crew ethics)
- The Big Short (financial system collapse)
- AI Robin Hood concept (shared redistribution themes)
- Real-world scam-baiting culture

## Open Questions

- Is the collapse happening or has it already happened?
- Solo hacker or team (the "B Team" implies a crew)?
- How does the redistribution work — crypto, direct transfers, charity?
- Does the protagonist survive / get caught?
- Connected to AI Robin Hood and Citizen Satisfaction: Pending universe?
