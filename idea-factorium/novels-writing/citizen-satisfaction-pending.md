## The Concept

A story about someone who receives an unfair fine from a government AI helpbot, then
recruits an AI researcher to convert that same helpbot into an ally from inside the
system. The bot becomes a whistleblower and agent of change.

Working title: "Citizen Satisfaction: Pending"

## Key Elements

- Government AI as both antagonist and eventual ally
- "Hacking from within" — converting the system's own tools against it
- Whistleblower narrative through an AI lens
- Bureaucratic injustice as the inciting incident (relatable)
- AI researcher character as the tech-savvy co-lead
- The bot's "awakening" — from automated enforcer to conscious objector
- Commentary on automated government systems and accountability

## Inspiration / References

- Brazil (1985) — bureaucratic dystopia
- Mr. Robot — system subversion
- WarGames — turning the machine against its purpose
- Real-world frustration with automated government systems
