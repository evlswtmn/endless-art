# Greg's Magic Book Adventure

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** novel
**One-liner:** A character finds a magic book with a bad spell that sends him traveling to different worlds.

## The Concept

A story about a character named Gene who finds a magic book with a bad spell that causes
him to travel to different worlds. An isekai/portal fantasy with the twist that the
transportation is accidental and the spell is "broken."

## Key Elements

- Portal fantasy / world-hopping
- The spell is broken — not working as intended
- Each world could be a different genre or setting
- Accidental travel creates comedy and danger
- The book itself as a character/MacGuffin
- Finding a way to "fix" the spell could be the overarching quest

## Inspiration / References

- The Neverending Story (magic book as portal)
- Sliders (involuntary world-hopping)
- Isekai genre (transported to another world)
- Discworld (magical mishaps)
