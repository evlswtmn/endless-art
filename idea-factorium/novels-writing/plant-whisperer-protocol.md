# Plant Whisperer Protocol

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** novel
**One-liner:** A factory chemist discovers houseplants that secrete mood-boosting chemicals — then corporations come hunting.

## The Concept

A near-future sci-fi short story. A P&G-style lotion department vet discovers houseplants
that secrete natural mood-boosting chemicals during a factory mishap. Turns into a thriller
when corporations hunt the tech for profit.

Planned as a quick 5k-word short for self-publishing on Amazon Kindle, with passive income
potential via serialized follow-ups.

## Key Elements

- Near-future setting — grounded sci-fi, not far-out
- Chemistry background gives it authenticity (draw on real production knowledge)
- Corporate thriller angle — the discovery attracts dangerous attention
- Sarcastic AI sidekick character (Douglas Adams-esque humor)
- The "magic" is chemistry, not fantasy — more Crichton than Tolkien
- Short enough to write fast, serializable for recurring income

## Inspiration / References

- Michael Crichton (science thriller with real-world grounding)
- Douglas Adams (humor and tone for the AI sidekick)
- Real-world P&G / chemical production knowledge
- The Martian (science problem-solving as narrative engine)
