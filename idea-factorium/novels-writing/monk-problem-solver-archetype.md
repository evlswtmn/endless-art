# The "Monk" Problem Solver Archetype

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** other
**One-liner:** The hero who wins not through violence, but by being so weird and technically correct that the villain just gives up.

## The Concept

A character trope discussion. The hero who doesn't defeat the antagonist through violence,
but by being so weird, specific, and technically correct that the antagonist gives up out
of sheer annoyance or respect for the craft.

## Key Elements

- Character archetype / writing tool
- Victory through pedantry, specificity, and sheer force of personality
- The villain's frustration IS the defeat
- Works best when the hero doesn't realize they're being annoying
- Combines competence with social obliviousness
- The "technically correct is the best kind of correct" energy as a superpower

## Inspiration / References

- Monk (TV series) — the namesake
- Columbo ("Just one more thing...")
- Shawn Spencer (Psych) — weaponized observation
- Jack Sparrow — wins through confusion
- Goku (sometimes) — too pure/dumb to be manipulated
