# Galactic Side Hustle

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** novel
**One-liner:** A hapless Earthling starts an interstellar newsletter empire from his garage, reviewing alien tech.

## The Concept

Full Douglas Adams vibes. A hapless Earthling starts an interstellar newsletter empire from
his garage, reviewing alien tech like mood-lotion dispensers. Chaos ensues with paranoid
android subscribers.

Designed as a funny short for Reddit or Substack, kept under 2k words for quick writes.
Bundle potential as an e-book ($4.99 passive sales).

## Key Elements

- Douglas Adams tone — absurdist bureaucracy humor, sarcastic narration
- Newsletter/review format could work as actual content (stories that ARE the newsletter)
- Paranoid android subscribers as recurring characters
- Corporate satire through an alien lens
- Short-form by design — quick to write, easy to consume
- Meta potential: the in-universe newsletter mirrors a real newsletter

## Inspiration / References

- The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy (obviously)
- Substack/newsletter culture (the thing being satirized)
- Red Dwarf (British sci-fi comedy)
- The Onion / Hard Times (satirical review format)
