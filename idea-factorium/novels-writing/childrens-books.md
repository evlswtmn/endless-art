**Status:** `idea`

**One-liner:** Whimsical children's stories incorporating tech and nature themes — high-volume output for a royalty stream.

## The Concept

Children's books — whimsical stories that could incorporate tech or nature themes,
reflecting a plant tech background. Aimed at high-volume output for Amazon KDP or
similar platforms to build a passive royalty stream.

Could be AI-assisted for illustrations or fully traditional — the volume play is the
key strategy.

## Key Elements

- Children's book format — short, visual, repeatable
- Tech + nature themes (plants that do things, friendly robots, etc.)
- Amazon KDP as primary distribution (low barrier, global reach)
- Volume strategy — many titles building cumulative royalties
- AI-assisted illustration as an option for speed/cost
- Personal knowledge of plants and tech gives authentic flavor
