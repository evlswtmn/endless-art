# Grounded Sci-Fi Short Stories

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** novel
**One-liner:** Sci-fi that focuses on the human element — family, mental health, and marriage in a high-tech future.

## The Concept

Grounded sci-fi short stories that focus on the "human" element — dealing with family,
mental health, and marriage in a high-tech future. Using real life experiences to give the
stories emotional weight that most "pew-pew" space operas lack.

## Key Elements

- Human-first sci-fi — the tech is backdrop, the people are the story
- Themes: family dynamics, mental health, marriage, caregiving
- Emotional authenticity from lived experience
- Short story format — publishable individually, collectible into anthologies
- Distinct from Plant Whisperer Protocol (thriller) — these are literary/emotional
- The kind of sci-fi that makes you feel something, not just imagine something

## Inspiration / References

- Ted Chiang (Stories of Your Life — emotional sci-fi)
- Octavia Butler (human experience in speculative settings)
- Black Mirror (technology's impact on relationships, but less cynical)
- Ray Bradbury (The Illustrated Man — short stories with heart)
- Real life — family, health challenges, love that persists
