# Immortal Couple Modern Life

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** webcomic
**One-liner:** A couple reincarnating together for 2,000 years — now neighbors who don't remember, but something feels familiar.

## The Concept

A webcomic about a couple who have been reincarnating together for 2,000 years (Vikings,
Samurai, Victorian England, etc.). In their current life, they are just regular neighbors
in an apartment complex who don't remember their past lives, but they experience intense,
unexplained deja vu and keep instinctively trusting/knowing each other.

## Key Elements

- Dual timeline: modern-day apartment life + flashbacks to past lives
- The romance is in the recognition — small moments of "I know you"
- Past lives span cultures and eras (Vikings, feudal Japan, Victorian, etc.)
- Neither character understands why they feel so connected
- Slow-burn modern romance layered with epic historical love story
- Visual style could shift between eras (modern clean line vs. historical painterly)

## Inspiration / References

- Your Name (connection across time/space)
- Cloud Atlas (reincarnation across eras)
- Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind (love that persists despite memory loss)
- The Old Guard (immortals in modern life)

## Open Questions

- Do they ever fully remember, or is the mystery the point?
- How are past life flashbacks triggered — objects, places, gestures?
- Is there an antagonist or obstacle, or is it purely a romance?
- Does anyone else in the modern world notice the connection?
- Art style — consistent or changes with each era?
- Webcomic pacing — weekly strips or longer chapters?
