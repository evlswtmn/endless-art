# The Manga Critic

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** webcomic
**One-liner:** A cynical art critic lives inside a shonen manga and writes scathing reviews of the fights happening around him.

## The Concept

A webcomic about a cynical, chain-smoking art critic who lives inside a shonen manga world.
While everyone else is hyped about power levels and transformations, he writes scathing
reviews about the lack of thematic cohesion and poor panel composition in the fights.

## Key Elements

- Meta-comedy — critiquing manga tropes from inside the manga
- The critic is powerless in a world of power-ups — his weapon is words
- Scathing, literate reviews of absurd shonen events
- Everyone else takes the world seriously; he's the only one who sees the "flaws"
- Could double as actual manga criticism/education disguised as comedy
- Chain-smoking curmudgeon energy in a bright, explosive shonen world

## Inspiration / References

- One Punch Man (character who doesn't fit the genre they're in)
- Gintama (meta-commentary on shonen tropes)
- Roger Ebert / Anthony Fantano (critic persona as entertainment)
- Deadpool (fourth-wall awareness, but more literary and less zany)

## Open Questions

- Does the critic know he's in a manga, or does he just think the world is poorly designed?
- Is there an overarching plot or purely episodic reviews?
- Does his criticism ever actually affect the world? (writes a bad review, villain loses power?)
- Visual gag potential — his reviews appearing as literal floating text panels?
- Could include real manga criticism lessons woven into the comedy?
