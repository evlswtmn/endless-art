# Lotion Lords of Golts

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** webcomic
**One-liner:** Factory workers battle corporate overlords with quirky inventions — sentient wipes, sci-fi puns, and hustle hacks.

## The Concept

A satirical webcomic strip about factory workers battling corporate overlords with quirky
inventions, like sentient wipes that predict mental health episodes. Grounded in real
chemistry/production knowledge for the "science."

Each episode ends with a "hustle hack" panel — a practical tip. Platform targets: Webtoon
or Tapas for free uploads, building toward merch (t-shirts with sci-fi puns).

## Key Elements

- Blue-collar comedy meets sci-fi satire
- Real chemistry knowledge gives the inventions authenticity
- Corporate vs. workers — David & Goliath with lotion
- "Hustle hack" panel at the end of each strip (practical + entertaining)
- Merch-ready characters and catchphrases
- Weekly drop schedule for audience building
- Mental health themes woven in naturally (sentient wipes as metaphor)

## Inspiration / References

- Dilbert (corporate satire strip format)
- Rick and Morty (science-driven comedy)
- Real factory/production experience
- Webtoon/Tapas webcomic ecosystem
