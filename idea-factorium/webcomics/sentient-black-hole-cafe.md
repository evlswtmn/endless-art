# Sentient Black Hole Cafe

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** webcomic
**One-liner:** A sentient black hole runs a cozy cafe inside its own event horizon, serving coffee to dead aliens and gods.

## The Concept

A webcomic set inside the event horizon of a black hole that has achieved sentience. The
black hole runs a small, cozy cafe. Its customers are deceased aliens and gods who have
been pulled into its gravity well.

The comic explores death, entropy, and serving coffee.

## Key Elements

- The black hole as a character — barista, therapist, gravity well
- Cozy cafe aesthetic inside a cosmically terrifying location
- Customers are dead gods, extinct alien species, collapsed civilizations
- Philosophical conversations about endings, entropy, time
- Warm tone despite the heavy subject matter
- Visual contrast: cozy cafe interior vs. warped spacetime exterior
- Each customer brings a different perspective on death and meaning

## Inspiration / References

- Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy (cosmic absurdity with heart)
- Death from Sandman (personification of a cosmic force with personality)
- Cafe/bar settings as framing devices (Cheers, Cowboy Bebop sessions)
- Interstellar (black hole visuals, time dilation)
