# Sci-Fi Sarcasm Tees

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** other
**One-liner:** Print-on-demand t-shirts blending sci-fi references with sarcastic hustle culture — "I, Side Hustler."

## The Concept

A t-shirt line with designs blending classic sci-fi references and sarcastic humor.
Examples: Asimov robots saying "I, Side Hustler" or Clarke monoliths as newsletter icons.

Printful for print-on-demand (zero inventory). Passive revenue via newsletter links and
cross-promo with other ventures.

## Key Elements

- Print-on-demand via Printful — no inventory, no upfront cost
- Sci-fi x hustle culture mashup designs
- Zero-effort fulfillment once designs are uploaded
- Cross-sold through newsletter, plant shop, and social media
- Design examples:
  - "I, Side Hustler" (Asimov robot)
  - Clarke monolith as newsletter icon
  - "Hallucination Hero" (personal/family humor)
  - "Plant Lord" (ties to Quirky Plant Emporium)

## Revenue Model

- Print-on-demand margins ($8-15 per shirt)
- Newsletter affiliate/direct links
- Bundle with plant orders for upsells
- Seasonal/limited designs for urgency

## Open Questions
