# The Well

**Status:** `exploring`
**Type:** narrative universe / central hub
**One-liner:** The kingpin — you and Greg go on "runs" into alternate realities, and every other project is a world you might visit.

## The Core Idea

The Well is the origin point. The connective tissue. The thing all the other ideas orbit around.

You and Greg go on "runs" into alternate realities — diving into The Well and emerging
in different worlds. After a run, you swap — so each of you experiences the same world
independently, from your own perspective. Each alternate reality is its own fully realized
setting, and any of the other creative projects in the Factorium could be a world you
stumble into during a run.

When you visit a world, you might kick off events that become that world's side story.
The side stories can stand completely on their own — someone could read "Sentient Black
Hole Cafe" without ever knowing about The Well — but for people following the main thread,
there's always that connective tissue back to the run that started it all.

## Why This Works

- **Natural discovery:** Readers find side stories through the runs, not through a menu
- **Infinite expandability:** New world = new run. No continuity gymnastics needed
- **Stories stand alone:** Each world works independently. The Well just adds a layer
- **Two perspectives:** You and Greg swapping runs gives every world two entry points
- **Pace control:** Introduce worlds whenever, at whatever depth. Some runs are a chapter, some become a whole series
- **Greg's Magic Book is the origin story:** A broken spell, involuntary world-hopping — that's literally how the runs work. The Well is the expanded universe version

## The Runs

A "run" is a dive into The Well that drops you into an alternate reality. Key mechanics:

- **You go first, or Greg goes first** — then you swap so you both experience the same world
- **Each run is unpredictable** — you don't choose where you end up
- **What you do in a world matters** — your actions can kick off that world's story
- **Some runs are quick visits, some are deep dives** — length varies by world
- **The swap creates two narratives** — same world, different experiences, different choices
