# Dover Dreamers Dispatch

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** other
**One-liner:** A Substack newsletter curating side hustles, sci-fi reviews, and local quirks — free tier hooks, paid tier pays.

## The Concept

A local-meets-niche newsletter on Substack curating side hustles, sci-fi reviews, and
Maryland/Dover-area quirks (e.g., plant-selling guides with production-inspired recipes).

Structure: Free tier for hooks and discovery, paid tier for exclusive idea sparks and
deep-dives. Snarky takes on failed hustles as a recurring feature.

## Key Elements

- Substack platform — low overhead, built-in monetization
- Three content pillars: side hustles, sci-fi, local flavor
- Free/paid tier split for growth + revenue
- Snarky, personal voice — not generic "hustle bro" content
- Affiliate links to eco-tech gear and tools for passive layer
- "Failed hustle" retrospectives as entertainment + education
- 1k subscriber target for sustainable passive income

## Revenue Model

- Free tier → audience growth and discovery
- Paid tier ($5-10/month) → exclusive content, idea sparks
- Affiliate links → eco-tech, books, tools
- Cross-promo with other ventures (Plant Emporium, tees, etc.)

## Open Questions

- Publishing cadence — weekly or biweekly?
- How personal does the voice get? (Family, work history, etc.)
- Does the sci-fi content include original fiction (Galactic Side Hustle crossover)?
- Local angle — is Dover-specific content a draw or a limiter?
- Growth strategy beyond Substack (X/Twitter, Reddit, local groups)?
