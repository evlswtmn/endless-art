# Quirky Plant Emporium

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** other
**One-liner:** An online shop for sci-fi themed houseplants — "Dune Succulents" that survive anything, with lore for every plant.

## The Concept

An online shop for sci-fi themed houseplants (e.g., "Dune Succulents" that "survive
anything"). Source from local growers, dropship for near-passive operations. Each plant
comes with a backstory / origin lore.

Cross-promote via the newsletter. Etsy for low-effort setup, add t-shirt designs like
"Plant Lord" for upsells. Growth strategy: go viral on X with funny unboxing videos.

## Key Elements

- Sci-fi branding on real houseplants — the theme IS the product
- Lore cards with each plant (ties creative writing to the product)
- Etsy storefront for low-overhead launch
- Dropshipping from local growers for near-passive ops
- T-shirt/merch upsells ("Plant Lord," "Dune Succulent Survivor")
- Funny unboxing content for X/TikTok virality
- Newsletter cross-promo (Dover Dreamers Dispatch)

## Revenue Model

- Plant sales (Etsy, potentially own site later)
- Merch upsells (t-shirts, stickers, lore prints)
- Content marketing → newsletter → more sales (flywheel)
- Wholesale/bundle deals for repeat customers

## Open Questions
