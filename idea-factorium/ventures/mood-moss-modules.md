# Mood Moss Modules

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** other
**One-liner:** Modular houseplant systems with sensors that monitor mental health and alert for wellness changes.

## The Concept

Modular houseplant systems with built-in sensors for mental health monitoring — alerts for
mood changes and potential psych episodes. Inspired by chemistry expertise and personal
experience.

Prototype with Arduino kits, pitch to Kickstarter. Long-term play: patent the
lotion-infused soil for passive licensing fees.

"Because who doesn't want plants judging your mood?"

## Key Elements

- Modular plant + sensor hardware system
- Mental health monitoring via environmental/biometric sensors
- Alert system for mood shifts or concerning patterns
- Arduino prototyping for MVP
- Lotion-infused soil concept (chemistry crossover — patentable?)
- Kickstarter for initial funding and validation
- Long-term: licensing the patent for passive income

## Revenue Model

- Kickstarter campaign for initial units
- Direct sales (own site or Amazon)
- Patent licensing for the soil/sensor tech
- Subscription for premium monitoring features (app-based)
- Partnerships with mental health organizations
