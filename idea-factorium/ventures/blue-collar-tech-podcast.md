# Blue-Collar Tech Podcast

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** other
**One-liner:** A podcast at the intersection of blue-collar expertise and high-tech side hustles — for calm, patient entrepreneurs.

## The Concept

A podcast focused on the intersection of blue-collar expertise and high-tech side hustles.
Building an audience of like-minded "calm and patient" entrepreneurs. Monetized through
sponsorships or related print-on-demand merch.

The angle: you don't need to be a Silicon Valley bro to build something. Real skills
from real jobs translate into real businesses.

## Key Elements

- Niche positioning — blue-collar meets tech entrepreneurship
- Authentic voice — not hustle-bro energy, calm and patient
- Guest potential — other tradespeople who've built side businesses
- Cross-promo with newsletter, merch, and other ventures
- Low production overhead — phone + editing software to start
- Sponsorship potential once audience builds

## Revenue Model

- Sponsorships (once audience hits critical mass)
- Merch cross-promotion (Sci-Fi Sarcasm Tees, etc.)
- Newsletter funnel (podcast → Dover Dreamers Dispatch)
- Premium episodes / Patreon for deep-dives

## Open Questions

- Platform — Spotify exclusive, all platforms, or YouTube-first?
