# Tech-Magic Hybrid

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** anime
**One-liner:** Magical energy treated like a utility — technicians keep enchanted infrastructure running while heroes get the glory.

## The Concept

A world where magical energy is treated like a utility — similar to how plant systems
are managed in the real world. Technicians (a magical version of a field tech) are the
ones who actually keep the "enchanted" infrastructure of a city running while the heroes
get all the glory.

Could work as anime or webcomic.

## Key Elements

- Magic as infrastructure — not swords and spells, pipes and grids
- Blue-collar magic workers — the unsung maintainers
- Heroes exist but they're not the focus — the tech crew is
- Direct parallel to real-world plant/utility work
- "What if your job was magical but still felt like a job?"
- Comedy and drama from the gap between glamorous heroes and working-class reality
- City-scale worldbuilding — how does a magic-powered city actually function?

## Inspiration / References

- My Hero Academia (superpowers as mundane in society)
- Dungeon Meshi (practical approach to fantasy)
- The DnD Adventuring Party Sitcom concept (blue-collar fantasy comedy)
- Real-world plant technician experience
- SimCity / Cities Skylines (infrastructure management as gameplay)
