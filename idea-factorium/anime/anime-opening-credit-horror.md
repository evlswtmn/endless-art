# Anime Opening Credit Sequence Horror

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** anime
**One-liner:** A character trapped in an infinite anime opening credits loop — existential dread meets J-Pop.

## The Concept

A short horror anime (or single scene) where a character realizes they are trapped inside
an infinite anime opening credits loop. The music repeats, they slide into frame over and
over, and they cannot leave the sequence or interact with the actual plot.

Existential dread mixed with J-Pop.

## Key Elements

- Meta-horror — aware of being inside an anime opening
- The familiar tropes of anime OPs become the horror (running toward camera, hair blowing in wind, dramatic sky shots)
- Repetition as psychological torture
- Music that's catchy but becomes sinister through repetition
- Character slowly breaking down while maintaining "opening pose"
- Could be genuinely unsettling despite the absurd premise

## Inspiration / References

- Groundhog Day / time loop horror
- The Backrooms (trapped in liminal spaces)
- Doki Doki Literature Club (meta-horror in familiar formats)
- Every anime opening ever (the tropes ARE the material)
- SCP Foundation entries (clinical documentation of impossible things)

## Open Questions

- How do you animate "being trapped in animation" — what breaks the fourth wall?
- Could this be part of an anthology horror series?
