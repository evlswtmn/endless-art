# Living Armor Romance

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** anime
**One-liner:** A suit of armor inhabited by a spirit falls in love with its wearer — told in both a sweet and sinister version.

## The Concept

Two versions of the same core premise — sentient armor and the person wearing it:

### Protective Version
A shy blacksmith creates a suit of armor inhabited by the spirit of an ancient knight. A
squire (or small-framed soldier) puts it on. The armor moves on its own to protect the
wearer — catches them when they fall, cooks for them. Slow-burn romance between the ghost
and the living wearer.

### Sinister Version
A lonely archaeologist finds a beautiful suit of cursed armor. She puts it on. The armor
keeps her safe, but also slowly erases her identity, trapping her soul inside with it.
A toxic, obsessive romance metaphor.

## Key Elements

- Central metaphor of intimacy — literally wearing someone / being inside someone
- Protective version: wholesome, gentle, slow-burn, cozy fantasy
- Sinister version: body horror, identity loss, toxic relationship allegory
- Both versions explore consent, boundaries, and what it means to "protect" someone
- Visual storytelling opportunity — the armor's body language tells the story

## Inspiration / References

- Fullmetal Alchemist (Alphonse — soul bound to armor)
- Beauty and the Beast (enchanted object romance)
- Parasyte (entity sharing a body)
- Gothic romance tradition (protective/possessive duality)
