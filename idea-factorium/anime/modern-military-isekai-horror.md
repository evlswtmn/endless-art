# Modern Military Isekai Horror

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** anime
**One-liner:** A US Navy carrier strike group gets isekai'd into a fantasy world and responds with quarantine protocols, not adventure.

## The Concept

Subversion of the "Log Horizon" genre. A US Navy Carrier Strike Group is isekai'd into a
generic fantasy world. Instead of adventuring, the Admiral declares the magic users a
"force of nature" and initiates a no-contact quarantine. The "heroes" are just terrified
sailors trying to figure out how to fuel nuclear reactors without a supply chain.

## Key Elements

- Military realism clashing with fantasy tropes
- No power fantasy — these are scared professionals following procedure
- The Admiral is pragmatic, not heroic — treats magic as a WMD threat
- Supply chain logistics as existential crisis (no uranium, no jet fuel, no GPS)
- Fantasy world inhabitants confused by people who DON'T want to be the chosen ones
- Horror comes from institutional helplessness, not monsters
- Chain of command and military protocol as narrative structure

## Inspiration / References

- Log Horizon / Overlord / generic isekai (the thing being subverted)
- The Pentagon Wars (military bureaucracy absurdity)
- GATE (military in fantasy, but played straight as power fantasy — this is the opposite)
- Tom Clancy-style military procedural tone
- The Terror (isolated military crew facing the unknown)
