# DnD Adventuring Party Sitcom

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** anime
**One-liner:** The Office meets dungeon crawling — a mockumentary about the HR nightmares of an adventuring party.

## The Concept

An anime/comedy set in a fantasy world that follows a party of adventurers, but the show
is structured like The Office or Abbott Elementary. It focuses on the HR nightmares of
dungeon crawling, inter-party dating drama, and the administrative bureaucracy of the
Adventurer's Guild.

## Key Elements

- Mockumentary format (talking heads, confessionals, shaky cam)
- Fantasy world played completely straight — the comedy comes from framing
- HR complaints about the barbarian's "workplace violence"
- Inter-party dating drama and breakup awkwardness during boss fights
- Adventurer's Guild as a corporate bureaucracy (forms, performance reviews, health & safety)
- Mundane problems in extraordinary settings

## Inspiration / References

- The Office / Parks & Rec / Abbott Elementary (mockumentary format)
- Konosuba (comedy adventuring party, but different tone)
- Dungeon Meshi (mundane meets fantasy, but more grounded)
- DnD actual-play culture (Critical Role, etc.)

## Open Questions

- Full anime series or could this work as a webcomic first?
- How deep does the "corporate" angle go — is the Guild a full-on corporation?
- Ensemble cast size — tight 4-person party or wider office cast?
- Does the show ever go "serious" or stay pure comedy?
