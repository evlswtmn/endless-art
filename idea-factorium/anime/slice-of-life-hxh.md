# Slice of Life Hunter x Hunter

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** anime
**One-liner:** Hunter x Hunter characters dealing with mundane life — Gon opening a bank account, Hisoka fighting library late fees.

## The Concept

A direct parody of Hunter x Hunter where all the characters are still incredibly powerful
and exist in the same dangerous world, but the plot is just them dealing with mundane life.

Gon trying to open a bank account. Killua doing grocery shopping. Hisoka getting upset
about library late fees.

## Key Elements

- All characters retain their full power and personality
- The world is still dangerous — it's just not the focus
- Comedy from the contrast between absurd power and mundane tasks
- Character-driven humor (Hisoka being Hisoka... at a library)
- Could work as shorts (5-10 min episodes) or full episodes

## Inspiration / References

- Hunter x Hunter (obviously)
- The Way of the Househusband (overpowered character doing mundane stuff)
- One Punch Man (power vs. boredom)
- Gintama (shonen characters in slice-of-life situations)
