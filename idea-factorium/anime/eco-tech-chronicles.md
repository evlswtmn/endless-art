# Eco-Tech Chronicles

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** anime
**One-liner:** A small-town family launches a plant-based tech venture that accidentally attracts interdimensional pests.

## The Concept

An original anime series following a family in a small town launching an eco-venture with
plant-based tech to combat climate woes, but it attracts interdimensional pests a la
Clarke's aliens. Episodic format with Asimov-style ethics debates woven in.

The wife is the real boss — hacking hallucinations into superpowers. Pitched as a concept
for fan art or scripting pilots to crowdfund on Kickstarter.

## Key Elements

- Family-driven story — not a solo hero, a household
- Small-town setting with global/interdimensional stakes
- Plant-based eco-tech as the MacGuffin and the solution
- Interdimensional pests drawn to the tech (Clarke-esque alien contact)
- Asimov ethics debates embedded in episodic plots
- The wife as the secretly OP character — practical genius
- Crowdfund-friendly concept (visual, high-concept, episodic)

## Inspiration / References

- Arthur C. Clarke (alien contact, technology as wonder)
- Isaac Asimov (ethical frameworks in sci-fi)
- Studio Ghibli (family stories, environmental themes)
- Steins;Gate (small-scale experiments with massive consequences)
