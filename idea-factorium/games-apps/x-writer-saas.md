**Status:** `idea`

**One-liner:** A content creation platform for X/Twitter writers — multi-account management, scheduling, analytics, team collab.

## The Concept

A content creation platform for X (Twitter) writers who want to manage multiple accounts,
schedule posts, analyze performance, and collaborate with teams. Solves the problem of
fragmented social media management.

## Key Elements

- Multi-account management
- Post scheduling and queue
- Performance analytics
- Team collaboration features
- Solves fragmentation of existing tools
- SaaS model — recurring revenue
