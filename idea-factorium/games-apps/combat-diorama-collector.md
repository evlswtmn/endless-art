# Combat Diorama Collector

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** game
**One-liner:** Collect detailed miniature figurines that wake up and fight when placed next to each other.

## The Concept

A mobile/PC game where players collect "diorama packs" containing highly detailed, miniature,
paintable figurines of warriors, monsters, and scenery. The twist is that when two dioramas
are placed next to each other on a virtual shelf, the figures "wake up" and fight in short,
automated 10-second battles based on their stats and positioning.

Focuses on collection, aesthetics, and passive strategy rather than active control.

## Key Elements

- Diorama pack collection mechanic (gacha-adjacent but focused on display)
- Highly detailed, paintable miniature figurines — warriors, monsters, scenery
- Virtual shelf / display system
- Figures animate and fight in short 10-second automated battles
- Battles determined by stats + positioning on the shelf
- Passive strategy — you set up, the game plays out
- Aesthetic-first design philosophy

## Inspiration / References

- Warhammer miniatures culture (painting, collecting, displaying)
- Auto-battler genre (TFT, Auto Chess) — passive combat
- Gashapon / blind box collecting
