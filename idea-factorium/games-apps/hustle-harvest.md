# Hustle Harvest

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** app
**One-liner:** A mobile app for tracking passive income ideas with AI tips tailored to your actual job skills.

## The Concept

A mobile app for tracking passive income ideas, like houseplant sales or newsletter subs,
with AI tips tailored to users' jobs (e.g., chem production hacks). Includes mood-tracking
for family health tie-ins.

Build plan: Use no-code tools like Bubble.io for an MVP. Beta test on Reddit's
r/sidehustle. Revenue goal: $100k/year via premium subs.

## Key Elements

- Passive income tracker — organize and track multiple revenue streams
- AI-powered tips personalized to user's professional background
- Mood/health tracking integrated (family wellness angle)
- No-code MVP approach (Bubble.io) for fast launch
- Freemium model — free tracking, premium for AI tips and advanced features
- Community angle — Reddit beta, user-shared strategies

## Inspiration / References

- Mint / YNAB (financial tracking UX)
- Side hustle culture (r/sidehustle, r/passive_income)
- AI coaching apps (personalized recommendations)
- Notion templates (flexible tracking)
