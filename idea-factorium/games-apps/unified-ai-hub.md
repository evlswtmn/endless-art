# Unified AI Conversation Hub

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** app
**One-liner:** One platform to combine conversations from all your AI services — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Kimi — into one place.

## The Concept

A platform to combine conversations from multiple AI services (Kimi, Claude, ChatGPT,
Gemini, etc.) into one unified interface. Access all AI conversations in one place, compare
responses across models, and maintain continuity across platforms.

Meta note: this project was literally born from the pain of having ideas scattered across
6+ AI chats — which is exactly what The Idea Factorium is solving with files.

## Key Elements

- Unified interface for multiple AI providers
- Conversation import/export across platforms
- Side-by-side response comparison
- Continuity — reference one AI's response in another
- Search across all conversations
- Could be the "hub" that replaces the current scattered approach

## Inspiration / References

- The Idea Factorium itself (solving the same problem with files)
- Franz / Ferdi (multi-messenger apps)
- Notion (unified workspace)
- The actual pain of having 6+ AI chat windows open
