# TimeVault / Historical Video Generator

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** app
**One-liner:** Enter a date and location on a globe, get an AI-generated video reenactment of historical events from that time and place.

## The Concept

An app where users enter a date and location on a globe, and AI generates a video
reenactment of historical events from that time/place. Could include "endangered history"
features to preserve native languages and cultural events.

## Key Elements

- Interactive globe interface — pick any point in time and space
- AI-generated historical video reenactments
- "Endangered history" preservation — native languages, lost cultures
- Educational and entertainment value
- Massive scope — every location, every era
- Could start focused (one region/era) and expand

## Inspiration / References

- Google Earth (globe interface)
- AI video generation (Sora, Runway, etc.)
- Historical documentaries / reenactments
- UNESCO endangered languages/cultures programs

## Open Questions

- AI video quality — is the tech ready for convincing reenactments?
