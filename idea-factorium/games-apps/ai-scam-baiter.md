# AI Scam Baiting System

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** app
**One-liner:** AI that wastes scammers' time and resources, potentially redirecting any "winnings" to charity.

## The Concept

Using AI to waste scammers' time and resources, potentially redistributing any "winnings"
to charity. Automated counter-scamming that scales beyond what individual scam-baiters can do.

## Key Elements

- AI-powered scam engagement (keeps scammers busy with fake victims)
- Time-wasting as the primary weapon (every minute with AI = minute not scamming real people)
- Potential charity angle for any money recovered
- Scales — one system can engage hundreds of scammers simultaneously
- Entertainment potential (like Kitboga but automated)
- Data collection on scam techniques for prevention
