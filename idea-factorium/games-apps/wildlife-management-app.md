# Wildlife Management App

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** app
**One-liner:** A private local AI system for wildlife management — small team, Telegram/WhatsApp integration.

## The Concept

A local AI setup for wildlife management purposes. Intended to be a private system with
limited access (3-4 people) using Telegram or WhatsApp integration.

## Key Elements

- Private / small-team access (3-4 users)
- Local AI deployment (not cloud-dependent)
- Telegram or WhatsApp integration for field communication
- Wildlife management specific workflows
- Low-user-count means simpler infrastructure
