# The "Plant Tech" Workflow App

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** app
**One-liner:** A utility app for field technicians — site logs, equipment tracking, and reporting, built by someone who actually does the job.

## The Concept

A utility app designed specifically for technicians in the field. Streamlines site logs,
equipment tracking, and reporting. Leverages 20+ years of professional expertise into a
"build once, sell many" SaaS model.

The key insight: existing tools are built by software people who don't understand the
actual workflow. This would be built by someone who's lived it.

## Key Elements

- Built from real field technician experience (not guessing at workflows)
- Site log management — quick, mobile-friendly entry
- Equipment tracking — what's where, what's due for maintenance
- Reporting — auto-generate reports from field data
- SaaS model — monthly subscription, recurring revenue
- "Build once, sell many" — one product serving an underserved niche

## Inspiration / References

- ServiceTitan (field service software, but bloated/expensive)
- Jobber (small business field service)
- The frustration of using tools not built for your actual workflow
- 20+ years of knowing what technicians actually need
