# Storyline to Comic Workflow

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** app
**One-liner:** An automated pipeline that takes a storyline, checks for plot holes, then converts it into comic panel format.

## The Concept

A workflow/system that takes a storyline, checks for plot holes and consistency, then
converts it into comic format. Could be automated or semi-automated.

Super relevant to the other creative projects — this is a tool that could produce
the webcomics.

## Key Elements

- Input: written storyline / script
- Step 1: AI plot hole and consistency checking
- Step 2: Panel layout generation
- Step 3: Art generation or art direction for each panel
- Semi-automated — human review at each step
- Could be the production pipeline for other Idea Factorium projects
