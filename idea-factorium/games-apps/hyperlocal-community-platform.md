# Hyperlocal News / Community Platform

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** app
**One-liner:** A curated digital space for the Eastern Shore (MD/DE) — local news, events, and marketplace without the social media noise.

## The Concept

A specialized digital platform for the Eastern Shore (MD/DE) that moves beyond noisy
social media groups. Curated local news, event tracking, and a community marketplace.

A "future-forward" local business play — solving the problem that Facebook groups and
Nextdoor are too noisy, while local news sites are dying.

## Key Elements

- Hyperlocal focus — Eastern Shore MD/DE specifically
- Curated news feed (not algorithm-driven outrage)
- Event tracking and calendar
- Community marketplace (buy/sell/trade, services)
- Cuts through the noise of general social media
- Could become a model replicable for other small regions

## Inspiration / References

- Nextdoor (local social, but too noisy)
- Patch.com (hyperlocal news, hit or miss)
- Facebook community groups (the thing being improved on)
- Local newspaper websites (the thing being replaced)
