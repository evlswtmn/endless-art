# Gen X AI Web App / Service Helper

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** app
**One-liner:** A service targeting Gen X users who need help navigating AI tools and modern tech — helper, not another platform.

## The Concept

A service specifically targeting Generation X users who need help navigating AI tools, web
services, and modern technology. Positioned as a "helper" rather than just another tech
platform.

The insight: Gen X is tech-literate enough to use tools but didn't grow up with AI. They
need a bridge, not a tutorial.

## Key Elements

- Gen X specific — not too basic, not too bleeding-edge
- "Helper" positioning — guides, not lectures
- AI tool navigation and recommendations
- Web service setup and optimization
- Could be an app, a service, or a content platform
- Pairs with the Gen X Newsletter concept for content marketing

## Inspiration / References

- AARP (age-specific service organization, but for a younger demo)
- Product Hunt (tech discovery, but overwhelming for non-techies)
- The "Gen X is the forgotten generation" cultural moment
