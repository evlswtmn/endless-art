# The "Drive to Survive" Effect Analyzer

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** app
**One-liner:** A data tool that quantifies how dramatized media increases real-world engagement with its subjects.

## The Concept

A conceptual data tool/app that analyzes media franchises (specifically sports media) to
quantify how dramatization and manufactured narratives increase actual merchandise sales,
ticket sales, and Google searches for the real-life subjects.

Named after the Netflix F1 series that demonstrably grew the sport's fanbase through
narrative-driven storytelling rather than pure race coverage.

## Key Elements

- Cross-references media release dates with sales/engagement data
- Tracks Google Trends, social media mentions, ticket sales, merch revenue
- Quantifies the "dramatization multiplier" on real-world interest
- Could apply to sports docs, true crime, historical dramas, etc.
- Data visualization dashboard

## Inspiration / References

- Netflix's *Drive to Survive* and its measurable impact on F1 fandom
- *The Last Dance* (Michael Jordan doc) and jersey sales spikes
- True crime podcasts driving real case interest
- Google Trends as a cultural barometer
