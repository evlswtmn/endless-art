# Elder Care AI Tools

**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** app
**One-liner:** A suite of AI tools for elder care — medication reminders, fall detection, companionship, family communication.

## The Concept

A suite of AI tools for elder care including medication reminders, fall detection, social
companionship, health monitoring integration with wearables, and communication tools for
family members.

## Key Elements

- Medication reminders (timed, smart, adaptive)
- Fall detection (wearable or camera-based)
- Social companionship AI (conversation, engagement)
- Health monitoring integration (wearables like Amazfit, Apple Watch)
- Family communication dashboard (alerts, status updates)
- Privacy-conscious design (it's watching Grandma, not surveilling her)

## Inspiration / References

- Medical Guardian (fall detection)
- Medisafe (medication reminders)
- Amazon Alexa Care Hub (smart home elder care)
- Amazfit GTR 4 health monitoring capabilities
