**Status:** `idea`
**Type:** other
**One-liner:** An educational e-course on Ancient Mesopotamia — historically accurate, engaging, with a premium monetization layer.

## The Concept

An educational resource on Ancient Mesopotamia developed into a monetizable e-course.
Balances historical accuracy with engaging presentation for modern learners, and free
access with premium content.

## Key Elements

- Ancient Mesopotamia subject matter (Sumer, Babylon, Assyria, etc.)
- E-course format — structured learning path
- Free tier for accessibility + premium tier for revenue
- Historical accuracy as a priority
- Engaging for modern learners (not dry academic content)
- Could use AI tools for visual recreations
- Connection to the TimeVault concept (historical visualization)
