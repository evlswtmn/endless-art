# endless-art

An art appreciation platform that displays random art in a unique way to encourage art appreciation and interest.

## Project Status

This project is in the **early development phase**. Tech stack decisions are being made.

## Vision

- Show random artwork to users in engaging, unexpected ways
- Encourage people to discover and appreciate art they might not normally encounter
- Create a unique viewing experience that differs from traditional gallery browsing
